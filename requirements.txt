1. Port Scanning Tool
A concurrent TCP port scanner that checks which ports are open on a target host and grabs service banners where it can.

2. Why I made openport?
I built this to understand how port scanning and service reconnaissance actually work under the hood, rather than just running tools like nmap without knowing what they do.

3. What it does?
By running the python script in the terminal with correct arguments(ex: python portscan.py scanme.nmap.org -p 22,80,443) it connects to the range of ports of list of ports given and outputs the port number and the banner.

4.Problems I hit:
-Banner grabbing depends on timing. When you connect to a port, some services (like SSH) send a little greeting — the banner — a fraction of a second after the connection opens. Your recv has to still be waiting at the moment that greeting arrives to catch it. There's a window, and if the window closes before the data shows up, you get nothing.
